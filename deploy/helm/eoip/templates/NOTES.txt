Notes / caveats:
  1. HugeGraph itself is NOT deployed by this chart. Point
     hugegraph.url (or neptune) at an existing backend.
     HugeGraph PD/Store distributed sharding (G2-SCALE-SHARD /
     GAP-PERF-001/002) requires HugeGraph enterprise/infra validation
     before production use.
  2. Worker HA relies on a filesystem-advisory leader lease
     (packages/eoip/ha.py). The persistence volume MUST be ReadWriteMany
     and shared by all worker replicas.
  3. HPA requires metrics-server in the cluster.
