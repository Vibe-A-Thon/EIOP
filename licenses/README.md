# License Documents and Notices

This directory contains supporting first-party commercial/distribution documents and third-party notice material. The repository's [licensing guide](../LICENSING.md) explains which document applies; [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) tracks dependency attribution and unresolved review.

Do not replace a dependency's license with EOIP's first-party license. Do not copy draft commercial terms into a customer agreement without the required owner/legal approval. SaaS and customer-installed distribution have different packaging and operational considerations; verify the actual offered artifact/profile.

When adding a dependency, model or copied asset, identify its provenance/version, applicable license text and any bundled components, update the notice inventory, and verify the resulting wheel/container/web/VSIX packaging. Keep existing license texts intact unless specifically correcting their provenance or approved terms.
