# Research Assets

`competitive/` holds structured competitor research records. [datasets](datasets/README.md) defines methodological separation and missing-score handling. Research is evidence about external claims or datasets, not proof that EOIP implements the capability.

Preserve source URLs, publication/access dates, methodology, uncertainty and versioned inputs when updating research. Distinguish primary evidence, inference, unknown values and unqualified comparisons. Do not merge distinct scoring frameworks or convert unknown scores to zero.

`tools/research/` contains research seed/validation helpers; inspect their effects before running them. Requirements distilled from research must have explicit owners and runtime acceptance criteria. Keep restricted invention material and any customer data out of public artifacts according to the repository's disclosure and licensing decisions.
