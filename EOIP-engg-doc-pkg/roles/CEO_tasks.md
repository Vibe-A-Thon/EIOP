## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| First-mover in unified cognitive intelligence platform | Large feature surface increases execution risk |
| 47 planned features create deep competitive moat | Single-developer dependency is critical risk |
| $14.4B TAM attracts investor interest | Enterprise sales cycles are 6-18 months |
| Multi-database support (17+) reduces vendor lock-in concern | Complex product requires significant customer education |

## Key Risks & Escalation Paths

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single developer / bus factor = 1 | Critical | Hire 2+ engineers immediately | Board-level discussion |
| Feature scope exceeds execution capacity | High | Ruthless phase prioritization (P0 first) | CTO + Delivery Manager weekly review |
| Enterprise deal cycle delays revenue | High | PLG (product-led growth) for Community edition | Adjust fundraising timeline |
| IP not protected before market entry | Critical | File provisional patents NOW | Legal Counsel immediate action |
| Compliance gaps block enterprise deals | High | SOC 2 Type II certification timeline | Security Officer + Legal Counsel |
