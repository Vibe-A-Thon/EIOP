## 1. Note on extra rows for strengths/inversions adopted without a dedicated requirement

The brief instructs adding a row (REQ-ID = "—", one-line reason) for any STR/INV card adopted without a dedicated requirement. Two sources were checked for this: `08-STRENGTH-ADOPTION-REGISTER.md` §1's 34-card "coordinator adoption shortlist (parity and exceed targets that drive the roadmap)" and `07-GAP-REGISTER.md` §3's 11 self-inversion cards (INV-SELF-01..11, one per forced-P0 gap). **Every card in both sets traces to a GAP-ID that already has a dedicated requirement below** — for example STR-mph-01 (metaphactory, GAP-14) folds into REQ-ING-02's row, STR-msgraphrag-01 (GAP-33) folds into REQ-QRY-07's row, and all 11 INV-SELF-NN cards are cited inline in their forced-P0 gap's row rather than broken out separately, because `07-GAP-REGISTER.md` §3 states they exist precisely to justify the P0 requirement already in that row, not as independent adoptions. This matches `11-REQUIREMENTS-FINAL.md` §5's own footnote that all 46 gaps converted to at least one requirement with none left over. **No extra "REQ-ID = —" rows are added as a result** — inventing one without a genuinely orphaned STR/INV card behind it would violate the no-fabrication rule this matrix exists to enforce. If a future pass finds an orphaned strength deeper in `08-STRENGTH-ADOPTION-REGISTER.md` §2's full by-category index (beyond the §1 shortlist checked here), it should be appended as a new row with REQ-ID "—" rather than retrofitted into an existing one.
